❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Find everything you need to know about our services.

How do I get the 10% discount on EICR? +
The 10% discount is automatically applied to all EICR certificate bookings this week. No code needed! Just book your inspection through our website and you'll see the discounted price at checkout.
What is an EICR certificate and why do I need one? +
An EICR (Electrical Installation Condition Report) is a legal requirement for all rental properties in England. It assesses the safety of your electrical installations. Landlords must have a valid EICR every 5 years.
What is the London Congestion Charge Zone? +
The London Congestion Charge Zone is a designated area in central London where a daily fee applies for driving. If your property is within this zone, an £18 surcharge will be added to cover our engineer's travel costs. You can check if your postcode is in the zone on the TfL website.
How quickly can I get an inspection? +
We offer next-day bookings across all London boroughs (subject to availability). Same-day emergency inspections are also available via phone or WhatsApp.
What property types do you cover? +
We cover all residential and commercial property types: Residential homes (with bedrooms), Block of Flats, HMO/Shared Houses, Commercial Premises, Office Buildings, Retail/Restaurant, and any other property type across London.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major payment methods: Pay Online (Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Stripe, Klarna), Bank Transfer, and Cash to Engineer. Online payments are processed securely.
